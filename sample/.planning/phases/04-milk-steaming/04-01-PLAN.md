# Plan 04-01: Steam Milk to Temperature

## Goal

The robot steams a jug of milk to 62 °C without scalding it or overflowing the
jug.

## Tasks

1. [x] Read jug temperature from the probe at 5 Hz
2. [x] Cut steam at the target temperature with a 1.5 °C overshoot allowance
3. [x] Abort and vent on a probe dropout

## Acceptance

- 20 consecutive jugs land between 60 °C and 64 °C
- No overflow at the 350 ml fill line
