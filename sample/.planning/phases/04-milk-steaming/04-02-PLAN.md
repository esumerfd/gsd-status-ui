# Plan 04-02: Pour a Drinkable Latte

## Goal

The robot pours steamed milk into a brewed cup and hits the 1:3 milk ratio.

## Tasks

1. [x] Track the cup rim with the wrist camera during the pour
2. [x] Ramp the pour rate down over the last 40 ml
3. [x] Weigh the finished cup and log the ratio

## Acceptance

- Ratio within 10% of 1:3 across 15 pours
- No spill outside the drip tray
