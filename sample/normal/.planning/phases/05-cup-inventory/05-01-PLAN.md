# Plan 05-01: Count Cups on the Shelf

## Goal

The robot knows how many clean cups are left without a human telling it.

## Tasks

1. [ ] Photograph the cup shelf on every kitchen visit
2. [ ] Count stacked cups from the side profile
3. [ ] Publish the count on the status topic

## Acceptance

- Count within ±1 cup of a hand tally across 30 visits
- A missing shelf reads as unknown, never as zero
